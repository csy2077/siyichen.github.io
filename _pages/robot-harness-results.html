How to read this archive

These are diagnostic runs, not a RoboLab benchmark or a policy success-rate estimate. The project did not have expert-replay seed qualification, so an official failure cannot be attributed solely to the policy. “No verdict” is distinct from failure. Only the selected environment's official task predicate supplies a success or failure verdict.

The MP4s show original observation frames at recorded boundaries. Motion between observations was not captured continuously. The evidence index records each exact run ID, source commit, seal hash, official outcome, and video hash.
