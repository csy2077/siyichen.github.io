About Me
======
* Education
  * I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the [University of Michigan, Ann Arbor](https://umich.edu/) (since Jan 2026), advised by [Prof. Jun Gao](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~jungao/) and [Prof. Qing Qu](https://qingqu.engin.umich.edu/). Before joining UMich, I received my B.S. from the School of Physics, [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/) in 2025.

* Research Interests
  * My current research directions include video diffusion distillation, VLM harnesses for robots, and LLM harnesses for scientific problems. Previously I worked on probabilistic machine learning, diffusion models, computational imaging and inverse problems.
